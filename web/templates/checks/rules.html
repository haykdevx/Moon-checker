What each collector inspects, what can look like a hit without being one, and what it cannot see. A collector's blind spots are why “no evidence found” is never proof that a PC is clean.

How outcomes are decided

Validated detection
At least one exact detection: a file whose SHA-256 equals a classified sample. Nothing else can produce it.
Review required
Indicators or concealment of medium severity or higher. Traces of one subject seen by several collectors are one reason, not several.
Unsupported configuration
The OS/architecture is not one the checker is validated on (supported: Windows 10/11 x64, Linux x86-64 with /proc).
Incomplete scan
A required collector failed, timed out, was skipped, or the checker lacked administrator/root rights.
No evidence found
Every required collector completed and nothing needs review.

Security settings (test signing, weakened boot integrity, virtual machines) never change the outcome; they lower the report's assurance. There is no score and no weighting.
